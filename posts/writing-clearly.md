Clear writing is not a gift. It is a practice. Anyone can learn it, and
everyone benefits from it.

## The enemy is clutter

Most bad writing is not wrong. It is cluttered. The meaning is buried under
extra words, passive voice, and vague nouns.

- Remove words that do no work.
- Prefer the active voice.
- Say the thing once, clearly.

## Before and after

**Before:** "It is important to note that the utilization of the tool was
found to be suboptimal in the majority of cases."

**After:** "The tool was slow most of the time."

## A checklist

- [x] One idea per sentence.
- [x] Short sentences.
- [ ] No jargon.
- [ ] Read it aloud.

## The payoff

> Writing is thinking made visible. When you write clearly, you think clearly.

That is the whole trick. Do the work, and the clarity follows.
