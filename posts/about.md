I am Santanu Sinha, a software architect who builds large-scale distributed
systems. I currently work as Chief Architect at [PhonePe](https://www.phonepe.com),
where I design the platforms that move money for millions of people every day.

## What I do

At PhonePe, I focus on the hard infrastructure problems that sit underneath
every transaction. I designed the central transaction processing platform that
handles tens of thousands to millions of financial requests per second at peak
scale. I also co-developed **Drove**, PhonePe's in-house container orchestrator
that powers services across data centers and the cloud, and architected
**Spyglass**, a graph-based automated root cause analysis tool for high-scale
microservices.

## Before PhonePe

My path to distributed systems ran through some of India's most demanding
engineering environments:

- **Ola** — Architect. I built the Fabric stream processing engine on
  Mesos/Marathon to handle real-time ride data.
- **Flipkart** — Software Architect. I worked on ebooks systems, the Foxtrot
  event storage platform, and the Ranger service discovery framework.
- **Interra Systems** — Senior Software Engineer and Technical Lead.

## Open source

I believe software should be shared. Over the years I have published and
contributed to a number of open-source projects on
[GitHub](https://github.com/santanusinha), including:

- **Drove** — a container orchestrator for high-scale microservices
- **Ranger** — a high-performance service discovery framework
- **dropwizard-db-sharding-bundle** — application-level database sharding for
  Dropwizard
- **hope** — a predicate expression evaluation language for JSON in Java
- **json-rules** — a JSON-serializable rule-matching engine using JSONPath
- **dropwizard-rabbitmq-actors** — a RabbitMQ actor system for Dropwizard

## Teaching

you to understand things deeply, and I still carry that discipline into my
engineering work.
